How it works

The maths, in plain words.

Each month the bank charges interest on what you still owe. Your EMI pays that interest first. Whatever is left reduces the loan.

That's why early EMIs are mostly interest, and why prepaying early saves the most.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n(1 + r)n − 1
P
Principal: the amount you borrow.
r
Monthly rate: annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100. 8.5% a year → 0.00708.
n
Number of months: 20 years → 240.
Example
₹50,00,000 at 8.5% for 20 years = ₹43,391 a month.
