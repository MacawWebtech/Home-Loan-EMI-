Service · Loan comparison

See every lender's real cost, side by side.

The advertised rate is one number. Fees, insurance, tenure limits and approval odds change what you actually pay. We compare all of it.

What's included

You share your income, existing loans and the property details. Within two working days you get a written comparison of 3–5 lenders that will realistically approve you.

Lenders checked30+
Turnaround2 working days

What we compare for each lender

  • Interest rate and how it's linked to the repo rate
  • Processing, legal, technical and stamp costs
  • Maximum LTV and tenure for your age and income
  • FOIR limits and how they treat bonuses or rental income
  • Prepayment and part-payment rules
