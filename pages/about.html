About us

We sit on your side of the table.

Ledgerline was started by a former bank credit head who saw too many buyers sign loans they didn't understand. We're independent, and we explain everything.

Our mission

Make every home loan a decision, not a gamble.

Most people spend more time choosing a phone than a 20-year loan. We want every borrower to know the total cost, the risks and the alternatives before they sign.
